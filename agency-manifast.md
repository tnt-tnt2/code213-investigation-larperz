MISSION:
 Investigate Operation Airport Snooper, a rogue Wi-Fi attack on a
 diplomatic laptop, and document how it happened and how to stop it.

INCIDENT SUMMARY:
 At 14:15 on Sunday, a senior trade delegate in the Terminal 1 VIP
 lounge at Houari Boumediene Airport (Algiers) joined an open network
 named FREE_VIP_AIRPORT_5G. Within twenty minutes the confidential
 file Trade_Agreement_Confidential.pdf appeared on a public paste site.

ATTACK TYPE:
 Evil Twin / Rogue Access Point

KEY EVIDENCE:
 - EV-01: The fake network had no password and the strongest signal.
   The official network, Aeroport_Alger_Public, needs SMS verification.
 - EV-02: The victim was redirected to http://192.168.1.1/login.html,
   a fake portal with an outdated airport logo asking for corporate
   email and password.
 - EV-03: Airport security saw a person near the gate with a battery
   pack and a small black directional antenna in an open shoulder bag.
